One-time setup

Connect your Spotify app

This tool talks to Spotify directly from your browser. You'll need to create a free developer app so Spotify trusts the connection. Takes about two minutes.

  1. Go to developer.spotify.com/dashboard and click Create app.
  2. Name it anything (e.g. "Playlist Rotator"). For Redirect URI, paste exactly:
  3. For APIs used, select Web API. Save.
  4. Open the app's settings and copy the Client ID. Paste it below.
